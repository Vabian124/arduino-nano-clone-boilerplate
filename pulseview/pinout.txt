Logic Analyzer	Microcontroller Pin	Why?
GND	Pin 8 (VSS)	Mandatory. This connects the grounds so both devices agree on what "zero volts" is.
1	Pin 5 (PA0)	I/O snooping
2	Pin 3 (PA1)	I/O snooping
3	Pin 2 (PA2)	I/O snooping
4	Pin 4 (PA3)	I/O snooping
5	Pin 7 (PA4)	I/O snooping
6	Pin 6 (PA5)	I/O snooping
7 & 8	Leave empty	We only have 6 signal pins to watch!
CLK	Leave empty	We don't need this. It is used for "synchronous" sampling where an external device dictates the timing. Your analyzer will use its own internal clock.
