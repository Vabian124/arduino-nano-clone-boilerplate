Remote color → key nibble (OR together when held)

  RED    bit0  0x1
  BLUE   bit1  0x2
  YELLOW bit2  0x4
  GREEN  bit3  0x8

Ring quarter starts (24 LEDs), after +180° then left 45° (index +9):
  RED    LED 9
  BLUE   LED 15
  GREEN  LED 21
  YELLOW LED 3
  (each quarter is 6 LEDs, wrapping)

Hold/release model:
  - HOLD while valid hex frames keep arriving
  - RELEASE when no valid frame for 150 ms (no solid-HIGH state machine)
  - Key mask needs 2 matching frames before UI follows

UI: active color quarters only; tap lamps 1/2/3; press ack LED
Gestures: short tap BLUE×3 = OFF, YELLOW×3 = ON (8s window)
