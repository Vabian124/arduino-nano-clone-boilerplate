Remote color → key nibble (OR together when held)

  RED    bit0  0x1
  BLUE   bit1  0x2
  YELLOW bit2  0x4
  GREEN  bit3  0x8

Ring quarter starts (24 LEDs), after +180° then left 45° (index +9):
  RED    LED 9
  BLUE   LED 15
  GREEN  LED 21
  YELLOW LED 3
  (each quarter is 6 LEDs, wrapping)

UI:
  - Hold: only active colors (no ghost quarters)
  - Key mask needs 3 matching frames before UI changes (stops RED+BLUE glitch → YELLOW)
  - Tap length excludes reconnect grace (fixes YELLOW x3 unlock)
  - Unlock/lock taps: single spaced lamps 1/2/3 in that quarter
  - Press ack: one bright LED on press

Gestures: short tap BLUE×3 = OFF, YELLOW×3 = ON (8s window)
